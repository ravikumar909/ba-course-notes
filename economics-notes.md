# Economics Notes — MDC Subject

    ## Unit 1: Basic Concepts

    ### Definition of Economics
    - **Adam Smith:** "Economics is the science of wealth"
    - **Alfred Marshall:** "Economics is the study of mankind in the ordinary business of life"
    - **Lionel Robbins:** "Economics is the science which studies human behaviour as a relationship between ends and scarce means which have alternative uses"

    ### Types of Economics
    1. **Microeconomics** — Study of individual units (firm, consumer, household)
    2. **Macroeconomics** — Study of economy as a whole (GDP, inflation, unemployment)

    ## Unit 2: Demand and Supply

    ### Law of Demand
    - As price increases → demand decreases (inverse relationship)
    - **Demand curve** slopes downward from left to right

    ### Exceptions to Law of Demand (Giffen Goods)
    - Price increases but demand also increases
    - Example: inferior goods during poverty

    ### Law of Supply
    - As price increases → supply increases (direct relationship)
    - **Supply curve** slopes upward

    ### Market Equilibrium
    - Point where demand = supply
    - Equilibrium price = market clearing price
